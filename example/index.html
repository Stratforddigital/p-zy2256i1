            Questions, answered

            How is the price worked out? Bedrooms plus bathrooms plus any extras you add. One bathroom is included, and there is no hourly rate.

            Do you add VAT? No. Silverpoint Cleaning Ltd is not VAT-registered, so the price you see is the price you pay.

            What do you bring? Everything: products, cloths, hoover and mop.

            Are you insured? Yes. Every job has public liability insurance and accidental damage cover.

            Who are the cleaners? Vetted local cleaners, interviewed and reference-checked. If you have regular cleans, you get a named cleaner.

            What if something isn't right? Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and put it right, free.

            Can I move a visit? Yes, free, up to 24 hours before.

            How do I pay? By card. You never pay cash to the cleaner.

            How do regular cleans start? Standard Cleaning starts with one deep clean, then weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits. Weekly and fortnightly save 10%; monthly is the standard price.

            When are you open? Monday to Sunday, 8am to 8pm.

            Rather talk it through? Call or WhatsApp us any day, 8am to 8pm.
